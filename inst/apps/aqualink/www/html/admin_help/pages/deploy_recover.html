Deploy / recover instruments

Choose the instrument, station, and deployment start. Link an installation or recovery visit at the same station when one was recorded. Under Equipment and recording settings, enter any deployment-wide firmware, logger program, and recording defaults you know.

Use Edit direct timeseries links for series recorded directly by the deployed instrument. Use Connection signals for logger channels. Both can appear on the deployment, and AquaCache checks for overlapping sources on a timeseries.
