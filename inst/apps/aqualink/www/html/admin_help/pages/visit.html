Add / modify field visit

Record the visit time, location, weather, instruments, photos, and notes. Instrument association is optional. After saving a visit, select it to manage its samples and field readings.

Samples and field readings

A field visit can be associated with several samples. Use Create a field sample with a measurement to make a sample and save its first field result together. Select a sample and choose Add a field result to record another measurement, including readings for an existing lab sample.

Use Associate existing sample(s) when lab samples were entered before the visit. You can link several samples to one visit. Each reading is stored once on the sample it describes; it is not copied to every sample linked to the visit.

Visit photos

Choose Yes for Were photos taken? and select one or more JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, or TIFF photos. Thumbnails are shown before saving. Photos are linked to the visit and use the visit start time and sharing groups. When modifying a visit that already has photos, they are shown as thumbnails; more photos can be added.

Adding samples from the discrete data page

The Add samples/results page supports manual entry and multi-sample uploads. Its optional field visit selection links every sample in that upload to one visit. Leave it blank for samples without a visit. Samples linked to a visit must match the visit's location and, when specified, its sub-location.
