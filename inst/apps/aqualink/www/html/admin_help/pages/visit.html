Add / modify field visit

Record the visit time, location, weather, instruments, optional photos, and notes. Instrument association is optional. In Add new mode, you can stage new field samples with measurements or associate existing samples before saving the visit. The visit and staged sample changes are saved together.

Samples and field readings

A field visit can be associated with several samples. Use Create a field sample with a measurement to make a sample and save its first field result together. Click a sample row to open its measurement editor, or select it and use Open selected sample. This works for new staged samples, associated existing samples, and samples on a saved visit. You can inspect saved measurements and add more field readings, including readings for an existing lab sample.

Use Add measurement to sample to build the preview, then save or stage the measurements from the modal. Staging does not write results to the database: the visit, sample links, and staged measurements are saved together when you save the new visit. Cancel closes the editor without applying its changes.

The editor blocks duplicate measurements in the preview. If an entry matches a saved result's full analytical identity (including qualifiers, method, and measurement time), it shows the saved and proposed values and asks you to confirm a replacement. A confirmed replacement changes only the result value, condition, condition limit, and result note; it preserves other metadata, sharing, and QA information. The preview labels replacements. If the saved result changes before you save, the save stops: reopen the sample, remove the outdated replacement from the preview, and enter it again to review the current result.

Use Associate existing sample(s) when lab samples were entered before the visit. You can link several samples to one visit. Each reading is stored once on the sample it describes; it is not copied to every sample linked to the visit.

Visit photos

Upload one or more JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, or TIFF photos if desired. HEIC/HEIF photos are also accepted when the server can convert them to JPEG, and are stored as JPEG. Thumbnails are shown before saving. Photos are linked to the visit and use the visit start time and sharing groups. When modifying a visit that already has photos, they are shown as thumbnails; more photos can be added.

Adding samples from the discrete data page

The Add samples/results page supports manual entry and multi-sample uploads. Its optional field visit selection links every sample in that upload to one visit. Leave it blank for samples without a visit. Samples linked to a visit must match the visit's location and, when specified, its sub-location.
