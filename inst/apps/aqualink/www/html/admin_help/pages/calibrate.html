Checks + calibrations

Save the basic calibration information first. Then open each applicable check or calibration and save that section, starting with temperature. A recorded temperature check is necessary for the calibration of other parameters, such as pH, ORP, and specific conductance, and also necessary to finalize the record. If calibrating conductivity/specific conductance along with pH or ORP, start with the former; pH/ORP calibration solution is highly conductive, and any traces of solution left over would affect the accuracy of the conductivity/conductance calibration.

Identify a replaceable probe

In each section, select the probe you tested by type and serial number. The list shows probes recorded in the holder's slots at the saved calibration time first. You can also select another registered probe for a bench check or when the slot history is incomplete. Leave the field empty for an integral sensor or if the probe is unknown.

To resume a draft, select it under View unfinished calibrations. Each saved section will show its recorded probe.
